A reference points from your device at knowledge that lives somewhere else: an old Hive, a vault, a wiki, a docs folder.

- Nothing is copied. The source keeps its own shape.
- Your Brainstem reads it as fenced, unattributed data and never runs it.
- To share part of it, you bring one signed copy into the Hive, stamped with where it came from.
