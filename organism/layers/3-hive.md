The Hive is where members share work. It comes in two kinds today, side by side:

- A **folder Hive** is markdown with git underneath and no owner inside. It is experimental, and an organization cannot bind one yet (G10).
- A **`rapp-hive/1` Private Hive** has one owner and signed RAPP/1 frames. It is in force, and it is the only kind of Hive an organization can bind, once an estate activates `rapp-work/1` (G16).
