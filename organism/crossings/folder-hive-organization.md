No specification lets an organization accept a folder Hive's state. `work.vector` records only an authenticated `rapp-hive/1` checkpoint, and the `work.*` kinds are closed. Two drafts in `kody-w/rapp-1` propose a way: a sibling profile whose folder-Hive organization records the accepted head in a signed vector (G10), with the owner signing as the Hive's notary (G5).
