The Brainstem app calls `agents/` plus Brainstem data the "RAPP Workspace". RAPP Constitution Article XVII calls `agents/` alone the user's workspace ("`agents/` IS the User's Workspace"), and Article XVI calls the Brainstem's scratch data "the brainstem's workspace". The Workspaces part means private workspaces under RAPP Workspace/1, with the RAPP Work SDK beside them. It is like G11. One idea is to rename one of them, or to state how they relate.
