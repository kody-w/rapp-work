The Brainstem app calls `agents/` plus Brainstem data the "RAPP Workspace". RAPP Constitution Article XVII calls `agents/` alone the user's workspace ("`agents/` IS the User's Workspace"), and Article XVI calls the Brainstem's scratch data "the brainstem's workspace". The Workspaces part means private workspaces under RAPP Workspace/1, with the RAPP Work SDK beside them. It is like G11. RAPP proposal 0010, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g22-workspace-names`, proposes a governing note: `agents/` is the Brainstem's agents folder, a RAPP Workspace is only a RAPP Workspace/1 workspace, and Article XVI's workspace is Brainstem data. It is not accepted: RAPP reserves constitution merges for the maintainer.
