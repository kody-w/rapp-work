SDK migration refuses a Hive that was seeded from a repository, and refuses world ids over 64 characters. That cap lives in `rapp-hive/1`'s `schema.json` (`$defs/label`) and in the SDK's `migration.py`. Proposal 0004 in `kody-w/rapp-work`, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g4-migration-successors`, adds an opt-in pointer-only successor, which records the source's world id as written, up to 128 characters; `rapp-hive/1` keeps its cap. It is not accepted.
