RAPP/1 lets an estate declare which Grail kernel each release scope pins, with a signed `grail-kernel` registry entry. No estate does this for the Brainstem yet. Its kernel has two channels, by design: LTS, which RAPP pins in its unsigned `KERNEL_PIN.json` (`kody-w/rapp-installer@brainstem-v0.6.9`), and the newest `brainstem-v*` tag (`brainstem-v0.6.16`). The estate kit prepared both scopes there, unsigned: candidate release manifests for LTS (`rapp-1-lts-2026.09`, pinning 291 certified repositories and leaving 26 out) and for the newest kernel. Only the estate owner can sign their `grail-kernel` entries, with rev-17's `release-pin` entries, as `registry_seq` 3 of the kody-w estate's registry. This is a drift finding.
