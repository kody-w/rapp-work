Once a `rapp-hive/1` roster is declared, nobody can be added or removed. Proposal 0001 in `kody-w/rapp-work`, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g1-roster-declaration`, lets the owner sign a later declaration. It is not accepted: the signed registry pins the specification's hash, so the owner must accept the text and sign again.
