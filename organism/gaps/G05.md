Canonical `rapp-work/1` gives an organization exactly one accountable owner, so a group of equals has no natural fit. Proposal 0005 in `kody-w/rapp-1`, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g5-owner-notary`, makes the owner the Hive's notary: they sign at the edge only what the Hive already approved, and name that approval. It builds on G10's proposal. It is not accepted, and no specification provides notarizing today.
