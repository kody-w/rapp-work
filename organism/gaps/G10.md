---
id: G10
gap: An organization cannot bind a folder Hive
home: Canonical `rapp-work/1` §§2, 4
fix: Name the Hive by its id, first commit and founder key fingerprint. A `work.vector` names an accepted head, which must come after the previous one
status: proposed here
---
An organization has exactly one Hive, but it has no way to name a folder Hive. The proposed fix names the Hive by its id, first commit and founder key fingerprint; each `work.vector` then names an accepted head that comes after the previous one.
