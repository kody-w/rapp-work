An organization binds exactly one Hive, and `work.vector` records only an authenticated `rapp-hive/1` checkpoint. So it has no way to name a folder Hive. Proposal 0010 in `kody-w/rapp-1`, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g10-folder-hive-binding`, leaves `rapp-work/1` as it is and adds a sibling profile, `rapp-work-folder-hive/1` (a working name). Its folder-Hive organization names one folder Hive by its id, first commit and founder key fingerprint, and each of its signed vectors names an accepted head that comes after the previous one. It is not accepted.
