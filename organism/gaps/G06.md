RAPP/1 lets an estate hand ownership to a new key, with a re-anchor record that the outgoing owner key signs (§§6.3, 13.2, 13.3). It cannot recover a lost or compromised owner root key inside the registry: that takes a new trust anchor, given out of band (§§13.1, 13.2). The rest of the gap is in the implementations: the `rapp-hive/1` reference fails closed on owner succession, and the SDK refuses owner rotation. Proposal 0006 in `kody-w/rapp-work`, a draft on branch `experimental/gap-g6-owner-succession`, implements succession in both. It is not accepted. Folder Hives need no owner.
